# Mentor session ratings — 19 November 2026

Update: [Explicit session ending](session-ending-verification.md) now allows a mentor to end a started call early and unlock the rating immediately. The original scheduled-end gating described below is historical.

Implemented the requested 1–5 star rating after a call. The learner sees it in My sessions once the scheduled end has passed and the mentor has marked the booking complete. The saved score replaces the form. The mentor directory, profile, and post recommendations show an average and count, or an honest no-ratings state. Ratings do not alter community upvotes or the recommendation ranking.

Migration `20261119073906_mentor_ratings.sql` is applied to the hosted project. Database types were regenerated. Only the booking learner may submit, through a checked RPC under a booking row lock. Booking-key uniqueness prevents duplicate ratings; identical retries succeed and changing a submitted score is rejected. Table writes are revoked. RLS limits individual records to booking participants; the public aggregate endpoint exposes only listed mentor IDs, averages, and counts, for at most 60 requested IDs.

Five database tests passed locally for lifecycle/identity, invalid scores and retries, direct-write denial and privacy, and aggregate correctness including unlisted/empty mentors. A sixth test exercises independent concurrent submissions in PostgreSQL CI and deliberately skips on single-backend PGlite. The complete local suite passed 41 tests, with two deliberate concurrency skips; type checking, lint, formatting, and the production build passed.

Five actual hosted API checks passed for eligibility/empty summary, unauthorized/invalid submissions, retry immutability, individual-row privacy/direct mutation denial, and accurate public aggregation. The browser submitted a 5-star rating to an unrated temporary booking and displayed the saved score; the public mentor profile updated from one 4-star rating to a 4.5 average across two ratings. At 390 px there was no horizontal overflow, and axe reported zero violations and zero incomplete checks on the rating form. No browser errors were reported. A one-off viewport command EOF was checked by reading the resulting viewport and running successful browser diagnostics.

Temporary rating records, bookings, accounts, credentials, and browser profile were removed. Cleanup confirmed zero remaining temporary users/ratings and preserved all five existing demo accounts. No sample star ratings were added to the demo mentors.

The Google Meet integration remains external: Nexus does not verify attendance or detect when someone leaves a call. Existing manual completion after the scheduled end gates rating eligibility. Written reviews, rating edits, disputes, and moderation tooling are outside this feature.

Security advisors were reviewed. The narrow authenticated mutation and deliberately public aggregate definer endpoints are intentional and remain advisory findings, alongside the previously documented booking functions. No student or booking IDs are returned by the public aggregate.
